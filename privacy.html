Data handling

Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026

DreamTouch Local Worker is a private Windows application operated solely by the owner of DreamTouch ASMR. It has no external application users or public account registration. This policy describes its read-only Google OAuth and YouTube analytics connection and this public information site.

Access and purpose

The owner personally authorizes Google access in Google's consent screen. The application requests only youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It accesses the authorized channel's identity, channel statistics, video catalogue and metadata, publication and privacy information, and available aggregate Analytics and Reporting data.

Analytics can include views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, subscriber gains and losses, traffic sources, aggregate audience segments, retention curves, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate when supported and available. Current analytics inputs are aggregate reports, rather than individual viewer profiles. The integration does not request individual viewer names, email addresses or subscriber identities. Aggregation alone is not a guarantee of legal anonymity in every context. Missing metrics are not invented.

The data is used for private channel analysis and the owner's requested creative planning. Additional API-based derived metrics, predictive baselines or analytical records are subject to YouTube's applicable permissions; this policy and OAuth consent do not grant that additional permission. Original creative materials and reviews that do not rely on Google API data are distinguished from API-derived data. It is not used to sell advertising, target individual viewers or provide a public analytics database.

Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in an encrypted Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) vault associated with the owner's Windows account. The OAuth client configuration is kept separately from the public site and outside the application's source repository. Google API requests use HTTPS.

Analytics responses, downloaded report files, derived observations and decision records are stored in local files and a local index, with local backup copies for recovery. Those analytical files are not individually encrypted by the token vault; their protection depends on the owner's Windows account, file permissions, device security and any disk encryption configured on that device. DPAPI is not a guarantee against malware or another process running as the owner.

Analytical records support the owner's requested historical comparisons. YouTube distinguishes authorized statistical history from other API metadata: continued statistical retention requires periodic authorization and relevant video checks; other metadata must be refreshed or removed within its applicable 30-day limit. The local maintenance task runs daily when the owner's computer and session are available. It checks the registered learning store and backups, records results and flags required action. It does not automatically cover every older report or off-device copy. Saving a newer snapshot does not retire an older copy. The operator remains responsible for resolving overdue items.

Codex and off-device processing

The owner uses OpenAI Codex for requested analysis and creative reasoning. Selected analytics, derived observations and relevant analytical context can be included in Codex inputs and processed by OpenAI. Consequently, this application does not promise that all Google-derived data remains solely on the local computer.

OpenAI's handling and retention of that content depend on the applicable product, account terms and data controls. This policy does not assert that a particular provider retention or model-training setting has been verified for every account. Before sending Google-derived data, the operator must verify provider controls and terms that limit processing to the requested user-facing purpose and comply with Google's applicable data-use restrictions. Disclosure alone does not authorize a provider to use Google-derived data for unrelated purposes. The worker itself does not train or fine-tune a general-purpose AI model.

OAuth client secrets, access tokens and refresh tokens are not included in analytical reports or intentionally supplied to Codex, the public website or its hosting repository. The application does not sell Google user data or transfer it to advertising platforms or data brokers.

Limited use

DreamTouch Local Worker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data provided for AI-assisted analysis is limited to delivering the owner's requested analytical and creative-planning features, with the owner's consent. This statement is not a claim that Google has audited or certified this application.

Revocation and deletion

You can revoke the application's access at Google security permissions or Google Account connections. The application must not bypass revocation; subsequent API access requires a valid grant. Disconnecting the application does not delete existing YouTube videos or analytics held by Google.

For a local disconnect, deletion request or assistance, contact arsent678@gmail.com. Local safeguards support a reviewed deletion plan for identified API observations, raw responses, dependent analytical records and registered backups. Destructive execution requires the owner's approval; there is no unattended purge or public account-deletion service. A local disconnect also requires confirmed Google token revocation. A temporary connection failure alone is not proof of revoked consent.

YouTube's applicable deadlines require prompt handling: local revocation or a stored-data deletion request within seven calendar days, and external Google revocation within 30 days of revocation. These are operator obligations, not a claim that every copy is automatically erased. Original DreamTouch media and creative materials independent of API data are outside a Google-data-only deletion. Unknown copies need separate identification, and restored backups must not reintroduce deleted data. Local deletion cannot erase YouTube's records or provider-held conversations and backups; OpenAI and Google have their own retention and deletion mechanisms.

Correspondence and applicable data protection rights

If you email the operator, your address and message may contain personal information. They are used to answer and manage the enquiry and keep a necessary record of its resolution, rather than for a mailing list or viewer profiling. Where UK data protection law applies, the operator's legitimate interests in responding and administering the request must be balanced against your rights. You can use the contact above to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights, and can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Statutory rights and their applicable response periods are distinct from Google's API contractual deadlines.
