Data handling

Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026

DreamTouch Local Worker is a private Windows application operated solely by the owner of DreamTouch ASMR. It has no external users or public registration. Privacy enquiries and requests: arsent678@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

With the owner's Google authorization, the application uses YouTube API Services to access channel identity, channel and video information, and available aggregate Analytics and Reporting data. This includes views, watch time, viewing duration, subscriber changes, traffic sources, audience segments, retention, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate where available.

Historical comparisons and statistical analysis inform performance evaluation, estimates of future outcomes, content improvement, AI-assisted creative planning and future production decisions. The integration does not request individual viewer or subscriber identities, sell Google data or use it for advertising targeting.

The analytics connection requests only youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly; it does not authorize video changes or publication.

Storage, protection and retention

Data and analytical results are stored in local files with recovery backups. OAuth tokens are encrypted using Windows account protection; ordinary analytical files rely on device security and file permissions and are not encrypted by that token protection. API communications use HTTPS. Credentials and tokens are kept out of public pages and analytical inputs.

Historical analytics are retained while authorized and useful for these purposes, subject to YouTube's periodic authorization and video checks. Other API metadata must be refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Regular maintenance flags required action; the owner remains responsible for updates and deletion, including older copies and backups. Removal is reviewed, not an automatic purge.

AI-assisted processing

Selected analytics, analytical results and relevant context may be sent to OpenAI Codex for the owner's requested analysis and creative planning. This processing is not entirely local. OpenAI's storage, retention and other data handling depend on the applicable product, account terms and controls; no universal provider-retention or no-training guarantee is made. OpenAI may process information outside the UK; its UK/Europe privacy policy describes transfer arrangements. The owner must verify compatibility with Google's data-use requirements.

DreamTouch Local Worker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access or request deletion

Revoke access at Google security permissions or Google Account connections. For a local disconnect or deletion of stored Google-derived data, contact the operator above. Deletion covers identified data, related analytical results and backup copies; unrelated original creative assets are outside that request.

YouTube requires deletion as soon as possible: within seven calendar days of local revocation or a deletion request, or within 30 days of revocation through Google. These are operator obligations, not a promise of unattended erasure. Revoking access or deleting local records does not delete YouTube videos or provider-held records; Google and OpenAI handle their own copies under their policies.

Enquiries and applicable rights

Email addresses and messages are used to answer and manage enquiries and kept only as needed to resolve them and maintain a necessary record. Where UK data protection law applies, legitimate interests in handling enquiries must be balanced against your rights. Contact the operator for applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability rights.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Website and external services

This information site has no sign-in, data collection form, advertising or application tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting does not connect a YouTube account.

See the Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service and OpenAI Privacy Policy. Material changes to data access, purposes or sharing will be disclosed before changed use, with consent where required.
