DreamTouch Local Worker is a private Windows application used only by the owner of DreamTouch ASMR to understand channel performance, improve content and support AI-assisted creative planning and future production decisions.

This website provides application information. It offers no public service, visitor accounts or access to private channel data.

Channel insights

Authorized channel and video information, with available aggregate performance, reach and audience-retention reports from YouTube.

Informed decisions

Historical comparisons and ongoing analysis help the owner evaluate results, improve creative choices and plan future videos.

Access and privacy

The analytics connection uses YouTube API Services with only youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. These permissions do not authorize video changes or publication. The application's production workflow uses separate owner authorization for YouTube Studio.

Data is stored on the owner's computer with recovery backups. Selected information may also be processed by OpenAI Codex. Read the privacy policy for data use, protection, retention and deletion.
