DreamTouch Local Worker helps the owner of DreamTouch ASMR collect YouTube channel analytics, compare published videos and use evidence in creative planning.

This is an information site for a privately operated Windows application. It is not a public analytics service, and it does not offer visitors a sign-in, an account or access to channel data.

What it reads

Authorized channel and video information, aggregate views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers, traffic sources and available audience retention. Daily Reporting API reports may also provide thumbnail impressions and click-through rate when YouTube makes those reports available.

What it helps with

Historical comparisons, retention diagnosis, private analytical records and creative decisions for the owner's channel. Missing data and uncertain conclusions remain labelled as such.

Two Google permissions

The analytics connection requests only youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. These permissions do not allow it to upload, edit, delete, publish or schedule YouTube videos. It uses the YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs, including daily analytics report collection.

Any separately authorized video publication through the owner's browser is outside this read-only OAuth connection. Granting analytics access is not permission to publish content.

Local collection does not mean all processing is local. Tokens are protected on the owner's Windows device. Selected analytics and analytical context can be processed by OpenAI Codex to provide the requested analysis. The privacy policy explains local storage, off-device processing and revocation.
